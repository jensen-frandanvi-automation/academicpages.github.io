## Abstract

We explore composing parameter-efficient modules using arithmetic operations. Our work demonstrates that these modules can be combined through addition and negation to achieve new capabilities without full fine-tuning, enabling flexible and efficient model adaptation for various tasks.

## Key Contributions

- Novel framework for composing parameter-efficient modules via arithmetic operations
- Demonstration of addition and negation operations on model parameters
- Efficient method for achieving new capabilities without full fine-tuning
- Comprehensive experiments validating the composition approach
