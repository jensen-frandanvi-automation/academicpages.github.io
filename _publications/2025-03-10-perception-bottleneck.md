## Abstract

We investigate the perception bottleneck of vision-language models (VLMs) when applied to chart understanding tasks. Our study identifies key limitations in how current VLMs process visual chart elements and proposes targeted solutions to improve perception accuracy for chart-based reasoning.

## Key Contributions

- Comprehensive analysis of perception failures in VLMs for chart understanding
- Identification of specific visual elements that cause perception bottlenecks
- Proposed solutions to enhance chart perception capabilities
- Extensive evaluation on chart understanding benchmarks
