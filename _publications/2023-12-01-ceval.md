## Abstract

We introduce C-Eval, a comprehensive multi-level, multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models. C-Eval covers 52 subjects across four difficulty levels, ranging from middle school to professional certification levels, providing a holistic assessment of Chinese language understanding and reasoning capabilities.

## Key Contributions

- Comprehensive Chinese evaluation suite covering 52 subjects
- Multi-level difficulty design from middle school to professional level
- Holistic assessment of Chinese language understanding and reasoning
- Extensive evaluation of state-of-the-art foundation models
