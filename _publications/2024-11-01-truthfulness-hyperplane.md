## Abstract

We discover the existence of a universal truthfulness hyperplane inside large language models that separates truthful from untruthful representations. This finding enables more effective hallucination detection and mitigation approaches by intervening on model internals rather than just output probabilities.

## Key Contributions

- Discovery of a universal truthfulness hyperplane in LLM representation space
- Novel method for hallucination detection based on internal model representations
- Effective intervention strategy for improving model truthfulness
- Comprehensive experiments across multiple LLM architectures and tasks
