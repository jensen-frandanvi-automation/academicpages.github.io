## Abstract

We propose using in-context sharpness of inner representations as an alert signal for hallucination in large language models. Our approach provides a new perspective for detecting and mitigating hallucinations by analyzing the internal representation dynamics during generation, enabling more reliable model outputs.

## Key Contributions

- Novel metric (in-context sharpness) for hallucination detection based on inner representations
- Comprehensive analysis of representation dynamics during hallucination
- Effective hallucination mitigation strategy based on sharpness alerts
- Extensive evaluation across multiple tasks and model architectures
